About Ethical Threads

Many fashion companies market their products as sustainable, but consumers often have to search across many websites, reports, articles, and brand pages to understand whether a company is actually transparent. This app gives users a single starting point for brand research.

Project Purpose

The purpose of Ethical Threads is to make fashion sustainability information easier to find. Users can search for a brand and view public results about sustainability reports, labor practices, carbon emissions, and ethical sourcing.

Target Users

User Group How This Website Helps
Consumers Helps shoppers quickly research brand sustainability before buying.
Students Supports research about fashion, ethics, labor, and environmental impact.
Advocacy Groups Organizes public brand information in a visual and accessible format.
